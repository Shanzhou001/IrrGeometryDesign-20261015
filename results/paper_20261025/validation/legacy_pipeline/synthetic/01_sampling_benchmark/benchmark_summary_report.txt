[Sampling Protocol & Fairness Guarantees]
  1. Spatial Total Points N = 128; strictly synchronized actual sampling points m.
  2. Regular Decimation covers full space: i_k = int(k * N / m) for k=0..m-1.
  3. Pure Random Sampling uses Fisher-Yates shuffle, exactly m points without duplicate.
  4. Jitter Initial Sampling uses grid-partitioned jitter, exactly m points.
  5. Hybrid uses Metropolis acceptance & local perturbation, strictly preserving sum(mask) == m.
  6. Coherence Ratio Attenuation definition: 20 * log10(mu_regular / mu_opt) dB.
     (Note: Measures peak mutual coherence ratio attenuation, not direct total field seismic aliasing energy).
