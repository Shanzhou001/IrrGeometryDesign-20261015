  - Method Notes (not measured claims):
    1. Multi-scale & Directional Sparsity in Curvelet Domain:
       Seismic reflection events exhibit hyperbolic curvature and localized wave fronts. While standard
       2D Fourier bases disperse curved events across the entire (f, k) plane, discrete curvelet frames
       provide optimal, highly sparse representations of parabolic wavefronts with minimal coefficients.
    2. Spatial Linear Interpolation Warm-Start:
       Initializing the iterative solver with spatial linear interpolation eliminates severe high-frequency
       harmonic noise and spatial aliasing from raw zero-fill, providing a robust initial trajectory.
    3. Cosine Annealing Thresholding & Scale-Adaptive Wiener Shrinkage:
       Smoothly decaying the regularization threshold prevents Gibbs ringing artifacts and preserves weak
       deep reflection amplitudes while preserving coarse background scales and suppressing noise.
    4. Strict Observed Trace Consistency Projection:
       Exact replacement of known channels after each inverse transform guarantees zero distortion on
       measured physical data (maximum error strictly < 1e-5).
